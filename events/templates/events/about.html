About NYC Parks Events

A place to find free events in New York City's parks, and people to go with.

What you can do here

  • Find events

    Search by keyword or in plain language, and filter by date, time, borough, and category.

  • Register and plan

    Register for events, add them to your calendar, and keep track of them in My events.

  • Host your own

    Post an event, manage who's coming, and send updates to attendees.

  • Meet people

    See who else is going, message them, and find people with similar interests.

Where the events come from

Most events come from NYC Parks through NYC Open Data, and we sync them every hour. Members can also post their own events.

This is not an official NYC Parks website. Always check an event's official page for the latest details.

Who built it

NYC Parks Events is a course project by Team 5 at New York University, Fall 2026.
